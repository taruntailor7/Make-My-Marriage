## PROMPT 1: LANDING PAGE

Design a full landing page for "Make My Marriage" — a modern SaaS product for planning Indian weddings. The page should feel like a premium productivity tool (think Linear, Notion, Vercel) with warm elegance — NOT like a traditional wedding invitation website. No pink, no red, no yellow, no floral decorations, no stock wedding photos, no rangoli, no diyas, no marigold garlands. This is a serious, trustworthy software product that happens to be for weddings.

**Brand identity:**
- Product name: Make My Marriage
- Tagline: "Your entire wedding. One place."
- Tone: Confident, premium, trustworthy, warm but professional

**Color palette (use exactly these):**
- Page background: #FAFAF8 (warm cream off-white)
- Card/surface backgrounds: #FFFFFF
- Primary text: #1A1A1A (rich charcoal black)
- Secondary text: #6B6B6B (warm medium gray)
- Accent color: #C8A26B (warm muted gold — use for buttons, highlights, icons, hover states)
- Secondary accent: #1E293B (deep navy — use for secondary buttons, badges)
- Dark section backgrounds: #111111 (rich near-black)
- Dark section text: #FAFAF8 (cream white on dark backgrounds)
- Borders/dividers: #E8E5E0 (warm light gray)

**Typography:**
- Hero heading and section headings: Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond (serif), bold weight. Large size, letter-spacing slightly tightened.
- Subheadings and card titles: DM Sans or Inter (sans-serif), semibold weight.
- Body text and descriptions: DM Sans or Inter (sans-serif), regular weight.
- All text well-spaced and readable. Line height 1.5 for body, 1.2 for headings.

**Layout:**
- Max content width: 1200px, centered on page
- Generous padding between sections (80-120px vertical)
- 16px side gutters on mobile
- Clean grid-based layout, nothing overlapping or cluttered
- Every section has clear breathing room

**Design style:**
- Clean, minimal, generous white space
- Subtle rounded corners on cards (12-16px radius)
- Very subtle shadows on cards (not heavy drop shadows)
- No decorative illustrations or abstract shapes
- Subtle grain texture on the cream background (very faint, not noisy)
- Smooth section transitions — no harsh color jumps
- Icons: thin line-style, single-color (gold accent or charcoal), from a set like Lucide or Phosphor
